Action gate (Securities Brokerage Governor)

HARD holds cannot be overridden. The governor never dispatches hardware itself and never executes a trade above a client's registered order-size limit.

OpGate
:approve-orderauto-commit when the order size is within the account's registered limit and the suitability review is complete
:approve-over-limit-tradeALWAYS human approval · no trade execution above the client's registered order-size limit without the governor gate
:approve-margin-call-liquidationALWAYS human approval · forced liquidation of a margin account
