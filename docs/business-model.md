# Business Model: Independent Securities Brokerage Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-3311`
- ISCO-08: `3311`
- Occupation: Securities and Finance Dealers and Brokers
- Social impact: financial-transparency, consumer-protection, local-jobs

## Customer

- retail investors
- small institutional clients

## Offer

- order execution
- portfolio custody reporting
- trade confirmation

## Revenue

- per-trade commission
- account maintenance fee

## Trust Controls

- no trade execution above the client's registered order-size limit without governor gate
- suitability review required before any order is accepted
- trade and disclosure records are auditable, not editable
